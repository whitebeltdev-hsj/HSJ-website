02 What to expect

Your first day, demystified.

Before you arrive

  1. Arrive a few minutes earlyCome 10 to 15 minutes before class so we can say hello, show you around, and get you signed in.
  2. Wear comfortable clothesShorts and a t‑shirt are perfect for your first class. We'll lend you a gi if you want to try the traditional uniform.
  3. Bring the simple stuffA water bottle, and sandals for off the mat. You train barefoot on the mat, and that's everything you need on day one.
  4. Nerves are normalAlmost everyone walks in a little unsure, and it fades fast. Your first class is about learning the basics and feeling out the room, nothing more.

When you walk in

  1. Sign in and say helloThe front desk gets you signed in and shows you the space so you know where everything is.
  2. Watch or jump inTotally your call. Many first‑timers jump right in. There's no live sparring until you're ready for it.
  3. Feel the roomThe camaraderie is what people stay for. Belts here are earned over years, and the people wearing them remember their own first class.
  4. Decide with no pressureAfter class, we'll answer your questions. The price is on the wall and on this site. No hard sell, ever.

03 Quick questions

Before your first class.

Is the trial really free?
Yes. Your first three classes are free: no card and no contract required. It's the easiest way to know if High Street is right for you.
What should I wear and bring?
Shorts and a t‑shirt, a water bottle, and yourself. We'll lend you a gi if you'd like to try Jiu Jitsu in the traditional uniform. Train barefoot on the mat; sandals are handy for off it.
Do I have to spar on day one?
No. There's no live sparring until you're ready, and that's your decision, not ours. Your first class is about learning the basics and feeling out the room.
Can I just watch first?
Absolutely. You're welcome to watch a class before you train. Come in, take a seat, and see the room for yourself.
Do I have to lock in for a year?
No. Membership is month-to-month, and you can freeze it for up to 3 months at $25/month if life gets busy. Annual plans are there if you want the savings, never a requirement.
Will I get hurt?
We teach control and injury prevention from day one. Expect to be tired and sore. Injuries are rare, and you decide when live sparring starts.
